Springfield Waste Management Association

The Springfield Waste Management Association (SWMA) is a nonprofit organization responsible for overseeing the shared wastewater systems that serve homes in Springfield Estates.

SWMA focuses specifically on the neighborhood’s shared wastewater infrastructure. Its purpose is to coordinate maintenance, manage related expenses, and help ensure these systems continue operating safely and reliably.

The association is governed by a volunteer board of homeowners, with a focus on responsible planning, professional support, and open communication with residents.
